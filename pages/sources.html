NEPSE Official API

Primary source for everything live — market prices, index snapshots, company disclosures, floor sheets, and index history for the trailing year. Served by Nepal Stock Exchange at nepalstock.com.

socrateai — nepse-open-data

Open-source NEPSE dataset (index OHLC, stock OHLC, floorsheets). Supplies our sub-index and headline history across the 2000s, 2010s and 2020s where the official API only keeps the latest year.
